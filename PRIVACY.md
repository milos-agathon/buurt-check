# Buurt-check Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 7 May 2026_

This Privacy Policy explains how Buurt-check handles personal data across the website, web app, Android app, iPhone app, address searches, reports, purchases, and related support channels (collectively, the "Service").

## 1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies when you:
- visit the website or web app;
- use the Android or iPhone app;
- search for an address or postcode;
- generate, view, share, delete, or download a quick checklist, source briefing, pre-bid questions pack, or full dossier;
- make a purchase;
- contact support; or
- otherwise interact with the Service.

## 2. Data We Process

Depending on how you use the Service, Buurt-check may process the following categories of data.

### a. Address searches and property identifiers
- search query inputs, addresses, and postcodes;
- derived coordinates;
- BAG, VBO, or similar address-linked technical identifiers;
- lookup identifiers and report identifiers needed to reopen or generate the same dossier.

### b. Pre-bid source briefings, packs, and sharing
- confirmed address details used to start source checks;
- minimized source-run records, coverage states, source dates, limitations, and generated verification questions tied to the confirmed address;
- anonymous buyer keys used to bind briefings, paid packs, report snapshots, and delete/share actions in the same browser or app flow;
- scoped share-link metadata, including hashed share tokens, expiry or revocation state, and share scope; and
- email-share contact hashes only when you explicitly request email sharing and a keyed contact-hash secret is configured.

Raw email addresses are not stored for the fallback email-share flow. When a contact hash is stored, it is a keyed HMAC-style hash using a server-held secret, not a bare hash.

### c. Purchase and entitlement records
- report type, payment status, entitlement status, and purchase timestamp;
- provider references such as Stripe session IDs, Google Play purchase tokens, or Apple transaction IDs;
- limited billing metadata needed to confirm payment and unlock access.

Buurt-check does not store full payment card or bank credentials when payments are handled by external payment platforms.

### d. Web checkout identifiers
- an anonymous buyer cookie on web to reconnect checkout returns and buyer-bound entitlements in the same browser; and
- a short-lived return or recovery token used to restore the same report after a payment redirect is interrupted.

### e. Data stored only on your device
- recent searches and saved addresses;
- theme preference, tooltip state, and viewing-checklist state; and
- temporary session state used to recover report lookup, post-checkout export, or an interrupted billing flow.

### f. Optional analytics
- on the website and web app only, and only with consent where required, Google Analytics 4 may measure page views, CTA clicks, language toggles, and broad product-interaction events; and
- advertising-related features remain disabled and IP anonymization is enabled in the current implementation.

Pre-bid analytics events are restricted to allowlisted, non-identifying state and count fields. They must not include raw addresses, postcodes, BAG/VBO/PAND IDs, report IDs, share tokens, email values or hashes, coordinates, URLs with query strings, source text, or generated findings/questions.

### g. Optional diagnostics and error monitoring

If enabled for a release, Buurt-check may receive technical error reports, app version data, browser or device characteristics, and limited diagnostic metadata to investigate incidents and improve reliability.

### h. Communications
- support messages;
- contact requests;
- bug reports; and
- feedback you choose to send.

## 3. Data We Do Not Intentionally Request

Buurt-check is designed to minimize collection of unnecessary personal data.

The Service does not use named user accounts for core access, and does not intentionally request your precise GPS location, contacts, photos, messages, or advertising identifiers.

## 4. Why We Process Data

Buurt-check processes personal data to:
- provide address-based analysis and generate reports or exports;
- confirm purchases and manage buyer-bound entitlements;
- reconnect checkout returns to the correct report in the same browser or app flow;
- operate, secure, maintain, and improve the Service;
- detect abuse, fraud, misuse, and technical issues;
- respond to support requests and user communications;
- comply with legal obligations; and
- where permitted, measure product performance and usage.

## 5. Sharing of Data

Buurt-check may share relevant data with third parties only where necessary to operate the Service, for example:
- public data providers used to generate the dossier, such as PDOK, BAG, 3DBAG, RIVM, Klimaateffectatlas, CBS, Leefbaarometer, and BRO-backed services;
- public pre-bid pack source providers used to check source coverage, such as KOOP/officielebekendmakingen.nl, PDOK/Kadaster, RCE, EP-Online when configured, and RDW/NPR where enabled;
- Stripe for web checkout and payment confirmation;
- Google Play Billing for Android purchase validation;
- Apple In-App Purchase and related App Store services for iPhone purchase validation;
- Google Analytics 4 for optional, consented web analytics;
- optional diagnostics or error-monitoring providers where enabled;
- hosting, storage, infrastructure, or security providers; and
- legal, compliance, or fraud-prevention counterparties where required.

Buurt-check does not sell personal data, does not use personal data for third-party targeted advertising, and does not use advertising pixels for the pre-bid briefing or pack flow.

## 6. Legal Bases

Where the GDPR applies, Buurt-check relies on one or more of the following legal bases:
- performance of a contract;
- legitimate interests in operating, securing, and improving the Service;
- compliance with legal obligations; and
- consent, where required for optional analytics or similar processing.

## 7. Retention and Deletion

Device-local data such as recent searches and saved addresses can be cleared from inside the app or by clearing browser or app data.

Provisional defaults are 90 days for pre-bid briefings, paid packs, share metadata, contact hashes, and minimized source-evidence snapshots, and 180 days for operational audit logs. Report and payment references are retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to deliver purchased exports, handle refunds or disputes, prevent abuse, investigate misuse, and comply with legal obligations.

Buyer delete immediately revokes scoped share links, marks buyer-visible pre-bid outputs deleted, and prevents shared reads. Deleted source evidence is no longer returned except as a minimal non-identifying internal tombstone. If you want us to review, delete, or locate a record tied to a report or purchase, email `support@buurt-check.nl` with the report ID, or with the address you used and an approximate date. Where applicable, Buurt-check normally responds within 30 days.

## 8. Security

Buurt-check applies reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal data against unauthorized access, misuse, loss, alteration, or disclosure. No system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 9. Your Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to, or request portability of personal data, and to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

You may also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority, including the Dutch Data Protection Authority where relevant.

## 10. International Processing

Some service providers may process data outside your country of residence. Where required, appropriate safeguards will be used for such transfers.

## 11. Children

The Service is not directed to children, and Buurt-check does not knowingly collect personal data from children in violation of applicable law.

## 12. Changes to This Policy

Buurt-check may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version becomes effective when posted, unless stated otherwise.

## 13. Contact

For privacy-related questions or requests, contact `support@buurt-check.nl`.
