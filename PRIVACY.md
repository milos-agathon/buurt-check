# Buurt-check Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 20 April 2026_

This Privacy Policy explains how Buurt-check handles personal data in connection with the website, application, reports, address searches, communications, and related services (collectively, the "Service").

## 1. Who We Are

Buurt-check is operated by Milos Popovic ("we," "us," or "our").

## 2. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to personal data processed through the Service, including when you visit the website, search for an address, request a report, create an account, contact us, or purchase access to paid features.

## 3. Personal Data We May Process

Depending on how you use the Service, we may process the following categories of data:

### a. Information you provide directly
- name;
- email address;
- billing information and transaction metadata;
- account credentials or authentication identifiers;
- messages, inquiries, support requests, and other correspondence;
- any address, postcode, or location query you enter into the Service;
- feedback, survey responses, or waitlist submissions.

### b. Information collected automatically
- IP address;
- approximate location inferred from IP;
- browser type, device type, operating system, and language;
- referring pages, clickstream, timestamps, and usage events;
- crash logs, diagnostics, and performance data;
- cookie or similar online identifiers, where applicable.

### c. Information derived in connection with the Service
- generated reports and dossier outputs linked to a user session or account;
- feature usage patterns;
- preferences and settings;
- fraud, abuse, and security signals.

## 4. Why We Process Personal Data

We may process personal data for the following purposes:
- to provide and operate the Service;
- to generate address-based analyses and reports you request;
- to authenticate users and manage accounts;
- to process payments and maintain billing records;
- to respond to support requests and communicate with you;
- to improve, debug, secure, and monitor the Service;
- to prevent fraud, misuse, and unauthorized access;
- to comply with legal obligations;
- to enforce our Terms of Use and protect our rights; and
- where permitted, to send product updates, launch news, or other relevant communications.

## 5. Legal Bases

Where the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) applies, we rely on one or more of the following legal bases:
- performance of a contract or steps taken at your request before entering into a contract;
- compliance with a legal obligation;
- legitimate interests, such as operating, securing, improving, and defending the Service; and
- consent, where required by law.

## 6. Address Searches and Location-Related Inputs

Buurt-check is built around address and neighborhood analysis. When you enter an address, postcode, or location-related query, we process that input to generate outputs such as maps, scores, derived indicators, and dossier content.

An address may relate to a private residence and can therefore be personal data in some contexts. Please enter only information you are comfortable submitting through the Service.

## 7. Cookies and Similar Technologies

We may use cookies or similar technologies for essential site functionality, authentication, analytics, performance monitoring, security, and user preferences.

Where required by applicable law, non-essential cookies or tracking technologies will be used only with the appropriate consent.

## 8. Sharing of Personal Data

We may share personal data with:
- hosting and infrastructure providers;
- analytics, error monitoring, and security service providers;
- payment processors;
- email and customer support providers;
- professional advisers such as legal, accounting, or insurance advisers;
- authorities or other parties where required by law, court order, or to protect rights, safety, and security; and
- a buyer, investor, successor, or transaction counterparty in connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, restructuring, or sale of assets, subject to appropriate safeguards.

We do not sell personal data to data brokers.

## 9. Third-Party Services

The Service may rely on third-party providers, APIs, datasets, maps, geocoding, hosting, analytics, payment, and communication tools. Those providers may process data under their own privacy terms and data processing arrangements.

## 10. Data Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, including to:
- provide the Service;
- maintain records of transactions and support;
- resolve disputes;
- investigate misuse or security incidents; and
- comply with legal, tax, accounting, and regulatory obligations.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of data, the nature of your interaction with the Service, and applicable legal requirements.

## 11. International Transfers

Your personal data may be processed in countries other than the country in which you are located. Where required, we will take appropriate steps to ensure adequate safeguards for such transfers under applicable law.

## 12. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal data against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure. However, no system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 13. Your Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to:
- access personal data;
- correct inaccurate personal data;
- request deletion of personal data;
- object to or restrict certain processing;
- withdraw consent, where processing is based on consent;
- request portability of certain personal data; and
- lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

If you would like to exercise a privacy right, contact us using the contact details made available through the Service.

## 14. Children

The Service is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children in violation of applicable law.

## 15. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version becomes effective when posted, unless stated otherwise.
