# Buurt-check Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 20 April 2026_

This Privacy Policy explains how Buurt-check handles personal data in connection with the website, mobile applications, address searches, reports, purchases, communications, and related services (collectively, the "Service").

## 1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to personal data processed through the Service when you:
- visit the website;
- use the Android or iPhone app;
- search for an address or postcode;
- generate or access a report or export;
- make a purchase;
- contact support; or
- otherwise interact with the Service.

## 2. Data We Process

Depending on how you use the Service, Buurt-check may process the following categories of data.

### a. Address and search-related data
- address, postcode, and search query inputs;
- report identifiers or dossier references;
- address-linked technical identifiers used to retrieve or generate outputs;
- entitlement or purchase status linked to a report or export.

### b. Purchase and transaction data
- transaction identifiers;
- payment status;
- platform-specific purchase tokens or entitlement references;
- limited billing metadata needed to confirm payment and unlock access.

Buurt-check does not store full payment card details when payments are handled by external payment platforms.

### c. Technical and usage data
- IP address;
- device type, operating system, app version, browser type, and language;
- timestamps, diagnostics, crash information, and performance logs;
- security and abuse-prevention signals;
- cookies or similar identifiers where relevant.

### d. Communications
- support messages, contact requests, bug reports, and feedback you choose to send.

### e. Local device data
Where applicable, recent searches, saved addresses, preferences, or draft state may be stored locally on your device for convenience.

## 3. What Buurt-check Does Not Intentionally Request

Buurt-check is designed to minimize the collection of unnecessary personal data.

The Service does not intentionally require named user profiles for core report access, and does not intentionally request data such as your contacts, photos, messages, or precise GPS location unless a feature clearly requires it and you choose to provide permission through your device or platform settings.

## 4. Why We Process Data

Buurt-check processes personal data to:
- provide address-based analysis and generate reports or exports;
- confirm purchases and manage entitlements;
- operate, secure, maintain, and improve the Service;
- detect abuse, fraud, misuse, and technical issues;
- respond to support requests and user communications;
- comply with legal obligations; and
- where permitted, measure product performance and usage.

## 5. Analytics and Cookies

The Service may use essential cookies or similar technologies for functionality, security, and performance.

Where implemented, analytics tools may be used only in accordance with applicable law and, where required, only after the appropriate consent has been obtained.

## 6. Sharing of Data

Buurt-check may share relevant data with third-party service providers only where necessary to operate the Service, for example:
- payment providers and app store billing systems;
- hosting and infrastructure providers;
- analytics, diagnostics, and crash reporting tools;
- customer support and communications providers; and
- legal, compliance, or security counterparties where required.

Buurt-check does not sell personal data and does not use personal data for third-party targeted advertising.

## 7. Third-Party Platforms

Purchases, access, and entitlements may be processed through third-party platforms such as payment processors and mobile app stores. Those providers process data under their own terms and privacy policies.

## 8. Legal Bases

Where the GDPR applies, Buurt-check relies on one or more of the following legal bases:
- performance of a contract;
- compliance with legal obligations;
- legitimate interests in operating, securing, and improving the Service; and
- consent, where required.

## 9. Retention

Personal data is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, including to provide the Service, maintain purchase and support records, investigate misuse, resolve disputes, and comply with legal obligations.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of data and the platform or processor involved.

## 10. Security

Buurt-check applies reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal data against unauthorized access, misuse, loss, alteration, or disclosure. However, no system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 11. Your Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to, or request portability of personal data, and to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

You may also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

## 12. International Processing

Some service providers may process data outside your country of residence. Where required, appropriate safeguards will be used for such transfers.

## 13. Children

The Service is not directed to children, and Buurt-check does not knowingly collect personal data from children in violation of applicable law.

## 14. Changes to This Policy

Buurt-check may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version becomes effective when posted, unless stated otherwise.

## 15. Contact

For privacy-related questions or requests, use the support or contact channel made available through the Service.
