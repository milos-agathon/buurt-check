Buurt Check Privacy Policy

Buurt Check helps buyers evaluate Dutch properties with open data, 3D context, and exportable dossiers. This policy explains what data is collected, why it is processed, how it is shared, and how you can manage or delete it. It covers the web app, the Google Play Android app, and the App Store iPhone app.

Data we collect and process

Address searches and property data

When you type an address into Buurt Check, the search query is sent to our backend, which queries Dutch public-data services that power address lookup. When you select an address, we process the selected address, derived coordinates, Dutch property identifiers such as BAG or VBO IDs, and generated report IDs to assemble the dossier and exports you request.

Purpose: address autocomplete, dossier generation, export delivery, and service operation.

Purchase and entitlement records

  • Report and entitlement records: report ID, report type, payment status, entitlement status, and purchase timestamp when a dossier is bought.
  • Payment provider references: provider session IDs, purchase tokens, transaction IDs, or similar payment references returned by Stripe, Google Play Billing, or Apple In-App Purchase so we can verify and fulfil a purchase.
  • Payment credentials: Buurt Check does not collect your card number, bank details, or other payment credentials. Those are handled directly by the payment provider.

Purpose: payment processing, purchase fulfilment, refunds, fraud prevention, and legal/accounting compliance.

Data stored only on your device

  • Recent searches: recently selected addresses are stored in local browser or app storage for convenience. This is separate from the live search queries sent to our backend to return suggestions.
  • Shortlist and preferences: saved addresses, theme preference, tooltip state, and similar convenience settings may be stored locally on your device.
  • Temporary session state: viewing checklist state, pending purchase restore state, and report-to-address lookup state may be stored in session or local storage so the app can recover cleanly after navigation or an interrupted billing flow.

Purpose: app functionality and convenience. This on-device data is not used for advertising.

Optional web analytics

On the marketing website and web app, Buurt Check can use Google Analytics 4 to measure page views and high-level interaction events, such as CTA clicks, FAQ interaction, language toggles, and product usage flows, when you allow analytics. We configure this in consent mode with advertising-related storage and personalization disabled and IP anonymization enabled where supported.

Purpose: product analytics, reliability monitoring, conversion understanding, and UX improvement. Your analytics choice is stored in a cookie and browser storage so it can be applied consistently across buurt-check.nl and app.buurt-check.nl. This analytics setup is not used for targeted advertising.

Data we do not intentionally collect

Buurt Check does not require named user accounts and does not use your device GPS location. We do not intentionally collect contacts, photos, messages, advertising identifiers, or payment credentials.

Data security

All data transmitted between your device and our servers is encrypted in transit using HTTPS (TLS 1.2 or higher). Server-side records such as report metadata, entitlement status, and payment references are stored in systems used to operate Buurt Check and are limited to the systems and operators needed to run the service.

Retention and deletion

On-device data

You can clear recent searches and shortlist entries through the app's Settings screen. Other locally stored data, such as theme preference, checklist state, and temporary session state, can be cleared by clearing your browser or app data.

Server-side data

Report records, entitlement records, and payment references are retained for as long as reasonably needed to provide purchased exports, handle refunds or disputes, prevent abuse, and comply with legal or accounting obligations. Operational caches may expire sooner according to technical time limits.

How to request deletion

To request deletion of server-side data, email support@buurt-check.nl with the report ID shown in your export or purchase confirmation, or with the searched address and approximate purchase or usage date. We aim to respond to deletion requests within 30 days.

What may be retained

  • Deleted where possible: report metadata, entitlement records, and related cached service data that are no longer required.
  • Retained where necessary: transaction records or other data that must be kept for legal compliance, fraud prevention, dispute handling, or security.

Legal basis for processing

  • Contract performance: processing addresses and assembling dossiers to deliver the service you requested.
  • Legitimate interest: operating and securing the service, preventing abuse or fraud, and improving reliability.
  • Legal obligation: retaining records where required by tax, accounting, or other applicable laws.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, or portability of your data where applicable. Because the product does not use named accounts, we may ask for the report ID, purchase confirmation, searched address, or other supporting details to identify the correct records before acting on a request.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens) at autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl.

Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy to reflect changes in our practices or applicable law. When we make changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. For material changes affecting how we process your data, we will provide notice through the app before the changes take effect.
