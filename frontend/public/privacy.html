Buurt Check Privacy Policy

Buurt Check helps buyers evaluate Dutch properties with open data, 3D context, and exportable dossiers. This policy explains what data is collected, why it is processed, how it is shared, and how you can manage or delete it. It covers the web app, the Google Play Android app, and the App Store iPhone app.

Last updated: March 24, 2026

Data we collect and process

Location (approximate)

When you search for an address, Buurt Check sends it to the Dutch PDOK geocoder and derives geographic coordinates (RD New / EPSG:28992) to query neighborhood data sources. We do not access your device GPS location.

Purpose: app functionality (assembling the dossier).

Personal information

  • Address searched: the Dutch street address you enter. Purpose: app functionality.
  • Anonymous buyer identifier: a random token linking your browser or app instance to purchased exports. Purpose: purchase fulfilment, fraud prevention.
  • Report ID: a unique identifier for each dossier snapshot. Purpose: purchase fulfilment, export delivery.

Financial information

  • Purchase history: report ID, payment provider, provider reference token, entitlement status, and purchase timestamp. Purpose: purchase fulfilment, refunds, fraud prevention, accounting.
  • Payment credentials: not collected by Buurt Check. Handled entirely by Stripe (web), Google Play Billing (Android), or Apple In-App Purchase (iOS).

App activity

  • In-app search history: recent address searches stored locally on your device (browser storage or app container). Not transmitted to our servers. Purpose: convenience (quick re-access).
  • App interactions: anonymized event breadcrumbs (e.g. "checkout started", "dossier viewed") recorded via the Sentry SDK. Purpose: analytics, crash diagnostics.
  • Shortlist entries and preferences: saved addresses, theme setting, and checklist state stored locally on your device. Not transmitted to our servers. Purpose: app functionality.

App info and performance

  • Crash logs: stack traces and error messages. Purpose: app stability, bug fixes.
  • Diagnostics: browser or device type, OS version, app version, request context, and performance metrics. Purpose: service reliability.

Device or other identifiers

The Sentry SDK may collect a device or session identifier for crash grouping. We do not collect advertising identifiers or hardware serial numbers.

Data security

All data transmitted between your device and our servers is encrypted in transit using HTTPS (TLS 1.2 or higher). Server-side records (report metadata, entitlement status, payment references) are stored in a database accessible only to authorized systems. Access to production infrastructure is restricted to the developer. Sentry processes diagnostic data under its own Data Processing Agreement and security practices.

Retention and deletion

On-device data

You can clear all locally stored data (recent searches, shortlist, preferences, checklist state) at any time through the app's Settings screen or by clearing your browser or app data.

Server-side data

Report records, anonymous buyer keys, and payment references are retained as long as needed to deliver purchased exports and comply with accounting obligations (up to 7 years for financial records under Dutch law). Crash logs and diagnostics in Sentry are retained for 90 days.

How to request deletion

To request deletion of server-side data linked to your anonymous buyer key, email support@buurt-check.nl with the report ID (shown in your export or purchase confirmation). We will process deletion requests within 30 days.

What is deleted vs. retained

  • Deleted on request: anonymous buyer key, entitlement records, report metadata, cached dossier data.
  • Retained where legally required: payment transaction records (Dutch accounting law), server access logs (abuse prevention, up to 90 days).

Legal basis for processing

  • Contract performance: processing addresses and assembling dossiers to deliver the service you requested.
  • Legitimate interest: crash diagnostics, fraud prevention, service improvement.
  • Legal obligation: retaining payment records as required by Dutch accounting law.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, or portability of your data where applicable. Because the product does not use named accounts, we may ask for the report ID or other supporting details to identify the correct anonymous record set before acting on a request.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens) at autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl.

Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy to reflect changes in our practices or applicable law. When we make changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. For material changes affecting how we process your data, we will provide notice through the app before the changes take effect.
