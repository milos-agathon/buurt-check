Buurt Check Privacy Policy

Buurt Check helps buyers evaluate Dutch properties with open data, 3D context, and exportable dossiers. This policy explains what data is processed, why it is processed, and how the payment paths differ between the web app, the Google Play Android app, and the App Store iPhone app.

Last updated: March 23, 2026

What we process

When you search for an address, Buurt Check sends the address lookup to Dutch public data sources and to our backend so we can assemble the dossier. We process the address, the BAG identifiers linked to that address, coordinates, generated report IDs, and the risk and neighborhood datasets needed to render the dossier and exports.

The app does not require a user account for the MVP. Instead, the backend issues an anonymous buyer identifier and stores buyer-bound entitlement records so a purchased full dossier stays linked to the address and the browser, Android app, or iPhone app instance that initiated the purchase flow.

On-device data

The web app stores recent searches, shortlist entries, theme preference, checklist state, and non-sensitive report coordination data in browser storage so the experience survives tab changes and repeat visits. The Android app stores the same app data inside the Trusted Web Activity container. The iPhone app stores equivalent data inside the native `WKWebView` container, which is separate from Safari storage.

Payments and purchase records

The free interactive viewer and the quick brief export remain free. Paid unlocks apply only to the full dossier export.

  • On the web, checkout is handled by Stripe Checkout.
  • On Android distributed through Google Play, checkout is handled by Google Play Billing.
  • On iPhone distributed through the App Store, checkout is handled by Apple In-App Purchase.
  • Our backend stores the report ID, anonymous buyer key, payment provider, provider reference token, entitlement status, and purchase timestamp needed to fulfil the purchase and handle fraud, refunds, and support requests.

Buurt Check does not store full payment card numbers. Stripe, Google Play, and Apple act as the payment processors for their respective channels and handle the payment credential data.

Diagnostics and security

If Sentry is enabled, operational diagnostics such as browser information, request context, and error traces may be processed to detect crashes and investigate service failures. We also keep server logs needed for abuse prevention, rate limiting, and payment reconciliation.

Retention and deletion

Browser-stored data stays on your device until you clear it in the app or your browser. Server-side buyer, entitlement, and payment records are retained only as long as needed to provide the purchased export, comply with accounting obligations, prevent fraud, and handle refunds or disputes.

To request deletion of server-side data that can reasonably be linked to your anonymous buyer key, contact support@buurt-check.nl and include the relevant report ID if available.

Your rights

If GDPR applies to your use of the service, you may request access, correction, deletion, objection, or portability where applicable. Because the product does not use named accounts in the MVP, we may ask for the report ID or other supporting details to identify the correct anonymous record set before acting on a request.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to support@buurt-check.nl.

This policy covers the web app, the Google Play Android release, and the App Store iPhone release of Buurt Check.
