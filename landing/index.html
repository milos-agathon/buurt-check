Anyone buying a home in the Netherlands, especially first-time buyers and expats unfamiliar with the local market. Buurt Check gives you the context you'd normally only learn after years in a neighborhood.

Waar komen de data vandaan? Where does the data come from?

Uitsluitend van officiële Nederlandse overheidsinstellingen: het Kadaster (BAG), RIVM, CBS, Klimaatatlas, TU Delft (3DBAG), en meer. Wij voegen geen meningen of schattingen toe. Exclusively from official Dutch government institutions: Kadaster (BAG), RIVM, CBS, Climate Atlas, TU Delft (3DBAG), and more. We don't add opinions or estimates.
